Featured app

English Breakfast: Vocabulary

A focused vocabulary app for practical English — with levels, categories, favorites and personalized settings.

Why English Breakfast

English Breakfast turns English vocabulary into an effortless daily habit. Combining spaced repetition, hands-free audio, and an AI text scanner into one intuitive app.

🎧
Hands-Free Audio in Headphones +
Learn on walks, runs, or during commutes without touching the screen.
  • Hands-free auto-playback of words with intelligent pauses
  • Native translation first, recall pause, then crystal-clear English pronunciation
  • Plays in the background and works seamlessly with phone locked
  • Studio British & American accents for natural listening
🃏
3D Flashcards & 4 Practice Modes +
Proven spaced repetition algorithm paired with engaging practice formats.
  • Interactive 3D flip cards with swipe mechanics and phonetic transcription
  • Fast-paced timed Quiz to challenge recall speed
  • Pair Matching game to cement associations between words and meanings
  • Spelling Sprint to master correct writing letter by letter
📚
6,600+ Words & CEFR Levels +
Curated by international standards from Elementary to Advanced.
  • Full CEFR coverage: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, and C2 levels
  • Real-world modules: IT & Tech, Business, Travel, Slang & Idioms
  • Complete irregular verbs dictionary with all 3 forms
  • Conversational phrasal patterns for natural spoken fluency
📷
Smart AI Book & Text Scanner +
Snap a photo of any book page or paste text to extract new words.
  • Instant optical text recognition (OCR) from camera or screenshot
  • AI highlights unfamiliar words not yet in your vocabulary
  • 1-tap addition of new words directly to your study deck
  • Ideal for reading physical English books and web articles
🎙️
Pronunciation Coach & Offline Sound +
Speak into your microphone and receive instant accent feedback.
  • Real-time speech analysis with instant clarity scoring
  • Toggle between British (UK) and American (US) pronunciation
  • Core vocabulary audio works 100% offline without internet
  • Accurate IPA phonetic transcription for every word
🏆
Weekly Leagues & Habit Streaks +
Earn XP, keep your daily streak alive, and climb the leaderboard.
  • Weekly competitive leagues with learners from across the globe
  • Daily streak tracker to build an unstoppable study routine
  • Detailed stats: mastered words, cards reviewed, and time spent
  • Milestone achievements and podium trophies
📝
Personal Dictionary & Mnemonic Notes +
Add custom words, idioms, and your own mnemonic associations.
  • Add any custom word, phrase, or idiom in seconds
  • Attach personal memory hints, mnemonic hooks, and example sentences
  • Favorites folder for dedicated focus on tough words
  • Instant lightning-fast search across your entire dictionary
☁️
Cloud Sync & 26 Interface Languages +
1-click Google login, multi-device sync, and full localization.
  • One-click sign-in with Google — no tedious forms
  • Automatic cloud backup via Firebase Firestore across all devices
  • Interface and translations available in 26 world languages
  • Installable Progressive Web App (PWA) for desktop and Android
