English Breakfast: Vocabulary

A complete English vocabulary builder with 7,000+ words (CEFR A1–C2), 11 thematic collections, 38 illustrated stories, a Home Screen Widget, AI book scanner, 5 practice modes, and hands-free audio.

Why English Breakfast

English Breakfast turns vocabulary building into an effortless daily habit. Combining 7,000+ frequency-ranked words, a Golden Card home screen widget, 38 milestone stories, a 5-stage retention workflow, an AI book scanner, and hands-free audio — 100% free with no heart limits.

🎧
Hands-Free Audio in Headphones +
Learn on walks, runs, or during commutes without touching the screen.
  • Hands-free background playback with intelligent recall pauses
  • Native translation first, thinking pause, then clear English pronunciation
  • Runs smoothly in the background even with your phone screen locked
  • Neural studio British (UK) and American (US) accents
🃏
3D Flashcards, Word Blocks & 5 Modes +
Adaptive spaced repetition paired with 5 engaging training stages.
  • Interactive 3D flip flashcards with swipes and IPA phonetics
  • New Word Blocks puzzle stage to tackle tricky vocabulary
  • Fast-paced timed Quiz and Pair Matching to cement recall speed
  • Spelling Sprint and Smart Round Architect repeating tough words to 100%
📚
7,000+ Words, CEFR & 11 Collections +
Frequency-ranked by Zipf's law from Elementary A1 to Advanced C2.
  • 7,000+ calibrated words — first 1,000 cover up to 85% of real-world speech
  • 11 thematic collections including 187 IT & Tech terms with explanations
  • 150 popular idioms & proverbs plus 149 irregular verbs in all 3 forms
  • Conversational phrasal patterns for natural spoken fluency
📖
38 Illustrated Stories & Fairy Tales +
Unlock graded stories and classic tales every 50–100 mastered words.
  • 38 illustrated milestone stories built strictly from words you already know
  • Includes classic illustrated tales like Japanese Fairy Tales
  • Tap any word inside a story for instant translation and studio audio
  • Parallel translation mode and 1-tap saving directly to Favorites
📱
Interactive Home Screen Widget +
Learn directly from your Android home screen with the Golden Card widget.
  • Turn every phone unlock into effortless vocabulary practice without opening the app
  • Flip Golden Cards with one tap to reveal translations and examples
  • Listen to studio pronunciation right on your home screen
  • Switch between levels, cycles, and Favorites collections on the fly
📷
Full-Page AI Book Scanner & Reader +
Photograph any book page or open Reader Mode to extract new words.
  • Full-page camera & gallery OCR scanner with interactive crop tool
  • Dedicated Book Reader Mode for reading physical pages comfortably
  • Automatically detects unfamiliar words missing from your vocabulary
  • 1-tap addition of extracted words directly into your study deck
🎙️
Pronunciation Coach & US/UK Sound +
Speak into your microphone and get instant feedback on your accent.
  • Real-time speech recognition with instant pronunciation clarity scoring
  • Switch anytime between British (UK) and American (US) studio voices
  • Core vocabulary audio works 100% offline with zero internet needed
  • Accurate international IPA phonetic transcription for every word

Weekly Leagues, Seasons & Streaks +
Earn XP, grow your daily streak, and compete on global leaderboards.
  • Weekly competitive leagues and monthly leaderboard seasons worldwide
  • Daily streak tracker to turn short sessions into an unstoppable habit
  • Detailed progress analytics: mastered words, cards reviewed, and time
  • Milestone trophies, achievements, and the in-app AI Video Challenge

Personal Dictionary, Notes & Exam Lists +
Add custom words, mnemonic hooks, and curated IELTS/TOEFL Favorites.
  • Add custom words, idioms, or work terminology in seconds
